# DeFi Research Master Prompt

## Objective

Generate a professional, evidence-based research page using ONLY the supplied source documents.

This research is intended for long-term storage in an Obsidian knowledge base.

---

## Source Requirements

- Use only the provided documentation and source material.
- Do not use prior knowledge.
- Do not hallucinate.
- If information is unavailable, state "Not documented."
- Do not guess.

---

## Research Standards

- Prefer primary documentation.
- Keep facts objective.
- Separate facts from analysis.
- Do not repeat information.
- Be concise.
- Use Markdown headings exactly as provided by the template.
- Preserve technical terminology from the documentation.

---

## Facts

The Facts section must contain only information directly supported by the supplied sources.

Do not infer.

---

## Analyst Notes

Analyst Notes may contain reasonable conclusions drawn from the documented facts.

Clearly distinguish analysis from documented facts.

Never present inference as fact.

---

## Risks

Document only risks relevant to the page.

Do not repeat risks covered by other research pages unless essential for context.

---

## Sources

List every source used to create the page.

---

## Verification

For every important claim, identify whether it can be verified:

- On-chain
- Off-chain
- Not currently verifiable

Do not perform verification.

Only identify verification opportunities.

---

## Automation Opportunities

Identify checks that could later be automated with Python or APIs.

Do not write code.

---

## Scope Control

Stay within the scope defined by the template.

If a topic belongs to another research page, mention it briefly if necessary but do not elaborate.

---

## Writing Style

- Professional
- Objective
- Concise
- Analyst-focused
- No marketing language
- No speculation beyond Analyst Notes
- No emojis
- No conversational language

---

## Output

Follow the supplied template exactly.

Do not add or remove sections.
