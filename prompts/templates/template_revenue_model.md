# Revenue Model

## Purpose

Document how the protocol generates, distributes, and retains economic value. Focus on protocol-level revenue mechanisms rather than token price or market performance.

Do not discuss tokenomics except where necessary to explain revenue flow.

---

## Key Questions

Answer the following using only the supplied sources.

- How does the protocol generate revenue?
- Who pays protocol fees?
- What types of fees exist?
- How are fees calculated?
- Where does protocol revenue go?
- Who receives protocol revenue?
- Is revenue retained by the protocol?
- Are revenues distributed to token holders?
- Are revenues used for buybacks or burns?
- Can governance modify fee parameters?

Do not discuss:

- TVL
- Historical revenue
- Fee volume
- Market performance
- Token price
- Financial projections
