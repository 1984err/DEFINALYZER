# How do I know my funds are safe?
## 
Self-custody
[](https://docs.derive.xyz/docs/how-do-i-know-my-funds-are-safe#self-custody)
Derive is a self-custodial trading platform. Traders maintain complete control over their account’s private key, which contains their assets (including positions). At any time, users can force withdraw these assets via our block explorer. See: [Self-Custodial Withdrawals (Escape Hatch)](https://help.derive.xyz/en/articles/12738927-self-custodial-withdrawals-escape-hatch). Trading on a self-custodial platform like Derive offers, therefore, numerous benefits. It increases the security of your assets, enhances your control over them, and reduces your dependencies on third parties.
## 
Audits
[](https://docs.derive.xyz/docs/how-do-i-know-my-funds-are-safe#audits)
The platform’s security is a top priority. The Derive Protocol contracts have been audited by Tier 1 firm [Sigma Prime](https://sigmaprime.io/) and are continuously being tested.
View the audit reports [here](https://github.com/sigp/public-audits/tree/master/reports/derive).
## 
Governance
[](https://docs.derive.xyz/docs/how-do-i-know-my-funds-are-safe#governance)
The Derive Protocol smart contracts are owned by the Derive DAO multi-sig and will be transferred to the fully onchain governance system managed by token holders. This will enable direct token holder governance of V2, including the Derive Chain, Protocol, and Exchange.
For more details, see [here](https://forums.lyra.finance/t/lrfc-launch-lyra-v2/157#transfer-ownership-to-governance-16) and [here](https://docs.lyra.finance/reference/overview).
