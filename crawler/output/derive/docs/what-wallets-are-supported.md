# What wallets are supported? 
Derive supports wallets like MetaMaskRabbyCoinbase WalletFireblocksWalletConnect.
Please note that we currently do not support multisig wallets such as Gnosis Safe or smart contract wallets.

MetaMask

One of the most popular self-custodial crypto wallets is [MetaMask](https://metamask.io/), a browser extension available in Chrome, Firefox, and Brave.

## 
Coinbase Wallet
[](https://docs.derive.xyz/docs/what-wallets-are-supported#coinbase-wallet)
The [Coinbase Wallet](https://www.coinbase.com/wallet) app is a self-custodial crypto wallet. To connect, open Coinbase Wallet on your phone and scan the QR code.
