Airdrop Breakdown
[](https://docs.derive.xyz/docs/drv-token-launch#airdrop-breakdown)
The airdrop introduces a new supply of up to 7.71%77,114,554Users can opt to stake their DRV for a one-time 25% bonus to their airdrop.
The airdrop program ran for 8 months from May 8 2024 to Jan 13 2025 in 6 rounds. Users were rewarded with points based on the following protocol actions:
The airdrop rewards a fixed amount of DRV for each round pro-rata based on points earned. In addition to the points conversion, users receive extra DRV for reaching certain levels based on points earned each round. The distribution for levels is pro-rata based on the cutoff points balance for each major rank (e.g. Apprentice and Explorer, not Explorer I and Explorer II). For example:

## 
Migration Breakdown
[](https://docs.derive.xyz/docs/drv-token-launch#migration-breakdown)
55.38% of the total DRV supply or 553,815,216 DRV is allocated to migrating LYRA and staked LYRA token holders. There are over 100,000 LYRA holders eligible to migrate.
LYRA holders who staked from April 19 in advance of the LYRA snapshot (A.K.A. [holder points program](https://blog.derive.xyz/ldx-farming-program/)) on May 8 earned a share of 2,529,958 DRV tokens pro-rata based on staked-hours.
Additionally, LYRA holders who prestaked their migrating DRV balance in advance of the token launch on January 15 earned a one-time 2.5% bonus in their staked DRV balance. A total of 60.09% of the migrating LYRA supply was prestaked, resulting in prestake bonuses of 7,415,725 DRV.
This creates a total of 56.37%

How to Claim Your DRV
[](https://docs.derive.xyz/docs/drv-token-launch#how-to-claim-your-drv)
Check Your Allocation  
Visit the dApp to preview your allocation: **[derive.xyz/airdrop](https://www.derive.xyz/airdrop)**
Your allocation includes:
  1. Snapshotted LYRA
  2. Pre-staking rewards
  3. Migration bonuses
  4. Claim Options
    1. Claim DRV directly for flexibility and liquidity.
    2. Claim and stake as stDRV to unlock weekly rewards and governance rights.

## 
DRV Token Utility
[](https://docs.derive.xyz/docs/drv-token-launch#drv-token-utility)

25% of protocol revenue allocated for token buybacks.
Executed on Derive’s DRV-USDC spot market.
